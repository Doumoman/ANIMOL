# Art correction 02 — original identity audit

Reviewed: the five original layer atlases and the five previously delivered platform layers in `ANIMOL_Art_Revision_Batch01_v1`, full four-layer composites assembled without changing pixels, and both local attachments dated 2026-10-06. This is a visual audit, not an approval of the previous batch.

## Reference interpretation

- `image(20261006-043026).png` supplies **technique**: each object has a legible continuous dark silhouette, modest connected shadow planes, broad stable fill areas, and sparse structural seams. Its furnishings, character costumes and palette must not be transferred into ANIMOL.
- `image(20261006-043800).png` supplies **existing ANIMOL identity**: cream/white card and header centers, navy labels, teal/gold ornamental frames, thumbnails at the left, and the original bright five-theme architecture. Replacing the light card centers with navy centers and white labels changes a major established color role. Restore the light panel role using the existing allowed palette. Keep runtime text/progress separately bound to actual data.

## Global preservation rule

Do not trade architecture for cleanliness. Keep the original silhouettes, column centers, span widths, opening shapes, balustrade rhythm, banner profiles, roof curvature, pendant positions and material color roles. Clean scattered interior flecks **inside** those shapes. Keep a restrained connected shadow on an arch underside or column side instead of deleting every shadow or redrawing larger rounded supports. Preserve architectural joints and deliberate motifs; they are not automatically noise.

The original assets contain substantial stippling. Most v1 platform interiors are already much flatter. The request therefore requires precise inspection rather than claiming that every previously delivered bridge is still uniformly dotted. The original far/mid architecture must also be considered when the user refers to visible bridge/building legs.

## Outline acceptance criteria

- At native resolution, an edge should use an intentional sequence of horizontal/vertical runs. Remove accidental one-pixel hooks, doubled staircase corners and isolated pixels on the silhouette.
- Use a stable contour width per object/contour class. Do not infer that all thick structural trim is a defect; deliberately consistent two-pixel frame trim can remain.
- A shadow boundary should remain a connected plane that describes a side, underside, recess or overlap. It should not dissolve into alternating dots or sporadic small patches across a large material face.
- Use the existing Sweetie16 color roles. Technique reference colors are not new palette permission.
- Verify at native scale and nearest-neighbor enlarged scale, including the whole composition, bridge supports, leaf edges, arch openings, and rail/window alpha holes. Numeric palette membership or low component counts alone do not prove the result is clean or faithful.
