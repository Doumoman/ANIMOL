Rounded blunt corners with orderly symmetric steps. No isometric top planes, no gradients, no anti-aliasing, no dithering, no random dots, no texture-noise speckles, no jagged inconsistent curve steps, no doubled border seams. Keep readable function symbols; design stays inside its cell.
